V1 Promise

Gravity Canyon v1 is a hosted private-room playtest alpha. The core promise is reliable online 2v2 artillery combat with real people. 1v1 is supported for easier testing, but it does not replace the 2v2 target.

  • People can open a hosted URL without installing the project locally.
  • Players can create or join a private room by invite link/code.
  • Players can complete private-room 1v1 or 2v2 matches.
  • The 2v2 gold validation is four humans on separate connections completing a match.
  • The build is playtest-clear and shareable with invited friends, not public-launch complete.

Room Flow

Access And Join

  • Anyone with the URL can open the game. No login, whitelist, or access code is required in v1.
  • Invite links open a lightweight join screen with an auto-generated editable display name.
  • Rooms are ephemeral. A room stays alive while at least one joined player remains connected.
  • No database persistence is required for rooms, profiles, match history, rewards, or inventory.

Lobby

  • The earliest connected player is host. If the host leaves, host passes to the next earliest connected player.
  • Host can change pre-match settings only: mode, match length, and map.
  • Settings are locked once the match starts.
  • Ready is per connected player, not per seat.
  • Host Start is valid when required seats are filled and all seated players are ready.
  • If Start is pressed early, the lobby shows missing seats and/or unready players.

Seats And Names

  • Mode means combat slots, not human count.
  • 1v1 has two units total: Red 1 and Blue 1.
  • 2v2 has four units total: Red 1, Red 2, Blue 1, Blue 2.
  • One connection may control one or two same-team characters, but cannot control both teams.
  • All required seats must be filled before a match can start.
  • Lobby seats show team/seat, player display name, selected character, weapon summary, and ready state.
  • Display names can be edited in lobby before each match and are locked during active matches.
  • Display names use simple limits: max length, trimmed whitespace, no empty names, and safe rendering.

Spectators And Capacity

  • V1 room capacity is 8 joined room members total.
  • Unseated members in the lobby may passively watch if they were present before match start.
  • Passive spectators cannot emote, control units, change settings, ready, or join seats mid-match.
  • New visitors during an active match are not joined. They see a match-in-progress screen with retry/light polling.
  • Only seated players with valid reconnect tokens can rejoin an active match.

Match Rules

Area V1 Rule
Modes Private-room 2v2 and 1v1. 2v2 is the primary v1 promise.
Match length Host chooses best-of-1 or best-of-3 before match start.
Round flow Between rounds, show a short result screen, update score, reset terrain/spawns/HP, then continue.
Post-match Return to the same room lobby. Preserve seat ownership where possible and reset ready states.
Turn order Server generates one fair, team-alternating turn order at match start. It persists across all rounds.
Turn timer 30 seconds. Timeout skips the turn. Movement already committed remains committed.
Shot commitment Firing commits the turn immediately. There is no post-shot movement window.
Sudden death Predictable cycle-based HP pressure after a round turn limit. Terrain hazards are v2.

Disconnects

  • Seat ownership is tied to a temporary room/session reconnect token, not an account.
  • Disconnected seated players have a 3-minute reconnect grace.
  • If a disconnected player's turn begins, the game waits up to 5 seconds, then skips that turn.
  • If reconnect grace expires, that player's controlled seats forfeit/KO.
  • The match does not pause for disconnects.

Combat

Controls

  • Combat is keyboard-first.
  • Left/Right move continuously while held, constrained by movement range and terrain legality.
  • Up/Down adjust aim.
  • Hold Space to charge power; release Space to fire.
  • The HUD shows a live power bar while charging.
  • Mouse is used for lobby/UI and the social phrase popup, not combat aiming or firing.

Server Authority

  • Server owns final combat results: turn ownership, legal movement, shot validation, terrain result, damage, KOs, round wins, and match wins.
  • Client may render and animate for responsiveness.
  • Server validates active player, angle range, power range, weapon, and turn state on fire.
  • Full charge-timing anti-cheat, rollback, and advanced lag compensation are v2.

Terrain, Wind, And Gravity

  • Terrain uses server result authority. The server broadcasts crater/result updates for client rendering.
  • Gravity is constant across v1. Gravity Canyon identity comes from terrain, voids, arcs, knockback, and Void Drops.
  • Wind is server-owned RNG selected at the start of each round and remains constant for that round.
  • Wind values are tuning, but non-neutral wind should matter without preventing game progress.
  • No advanced wind bands, gravity wells, map-specific physics, or local gravity zones in v1.

Aiming And Camera

  • Aiming uses a broad Gunbound-style upper arc. Exact limits are tuning values.
  • No trajectory line, landing marker, auto-aim, target lock, or full shot preview.
  • Global tactical context beats cinematic camera.
  • The view should usually show the battlefield clearly like a classic artillery game.
  • Projectile follow is restrained and only for impact readability.

Damage, Hitboxes, And KOs

  • Direct-hit collision and HP damage use one shared fixed vehicle combat hull for all v1 characters.
  • Pilot art and cosmetic detail do not define damageable pixels.
  • Enemy units are valid direct-hit targets and can body-block shots.
  • Allied units do not block allied projectiles.
  • Allied units are immune to allied direct HP damage, splash HP damage, and weapon knockback.
  • Terrain deformation affects everyone, including allies.
  • Units can be KO'd by HP reaching 0 or by being Void Dropped.
  • Void Dropped means the unit falls below the playable battlefield/death plane into the canyon void.
  • Multi-KOs, self-KOs, and teammate Void Drops from terrain mistakes are valid.
  • Unsupported units fall/settle immediately after terrain destruction. No slope sliding and no fall damage.

HUD And Feedback

  • V1 uses a classic all-info-visible artillery HUD.
  • HUD must show turn order, active player/seat/character, team scores, round tracker, HP, wind, angle, power, movement, weapon info, and timer.
  • Feedback distinguishes Direct Hit, Splash Hit, KO, and Void Dropped.
  • When units overlap, the active/moving unit renders in front and gets the strongest active marker.
  • Duplicate characters are distinguished by player display name, team/seat label, HP/nameplate color, and active markers, not sprite recolors.

Roster And Weapons

V1 roster is exactly Nova, Vesper, Kaelii, and Perlah. Duplicate picks are allowed, including four of the same character. No new playable characters enter v1.

Character art may be refined only to reach a readable, consistent, playtest-ready baseline for the four v1 characters and their required runtime states.

Character Role Tag Working Weapon V1 Behavior Lock
Nova Terrain Canyon Breaker Single heavy crater shot with the strongest terrain carve and Void Drop setup potential.
Vesper Disrupt Gravity Well Gravity-tech pull field at impact that drags enemies toward the impact point.
Kaelii Trick Shot Skip Roller Terrain-following rolling projectile that punishes enemies stuck in craters or behind small lips.
Perlah Cluster Sunspike Cluster Impact-splitting cluster shot that creates compact small blasts for reliable area damage.
  • Weapon names are working names until final v1 doc approval. Behaviors are the design lock.
  • Each weapon gets one matching readable VFX and one matching non-voice SFX.
  • Damage, crater size, pull strength, roll duration, cluster count, knockback, wind bounds, and aim limits are tuning values.
  • No secondary weapons, passives, upgrades, ultimate attacks, cosmetic VFX variants, voice barks, or music in v1.

Maps And Social

Maps

  • V1 ships with 5 fixed maps.
  • Each map has a name/theme and fixed 1v1 and 2v2 spawn layouts.
  • Host can choose a specific map or Random.
  • If Random is selected, the server may choose a fresh map each round.
  • If a specific map is selected, that map repeats each round with fresh reset state.
  • No map voting, custom maps, procedural sliders, or player-placed spawns in v1.

Preset Phrases

  • No free-text chat in v1.
  • Seated players can click one of their controlled characters to open a small social popup.
  • The popup allows preset phrase bubbles only.
  • Phrase bubbles appear temporarily above the selected controlled character.
  • No phrase feed, phrase history, custom text, spectator emotes, or emote cosmetics in v1.

Testing And Acceptance

Automated Verification

Automated tests cover deterministic game rules and server-owned multiplayer state. Visual feel, art, SFX, camera readability, and fun are verified through manual smoke tests and real playtests.

  • Room creation/join and settings.
  • Seat claiming, same-team two-character ownership, and duplicate character picks.
  • Ready/start validation.
  • 1v1 and 2v2 setup.
  • Persistent match turn order.
  • Best-of-1 and best-of-3 scoring.
  • Movement legality basics.
  • Shot result, damage, KO, Void Dropped, and win resolution.
  • Reconnect token recovery and disconnect skip/forfeit behavior.
  • Map selection and Random map rules.

Acceptance Gates

Gate Requirement
Build Complete Automated tests and build pass. Controlled multi-client smoke tests complete the core room/match flow.
Playtest Accepted Real people complete supported private-room playtests and provide feedback.
Gold Network Validation Four humans on separate connections complete a private-room online 2v2 match.

Out Of Scope For V1

Good ideas belong here until v1 is accepted. Parking an idea preserves it without changing the v1 contract.
  • Accounts, login, durable profiles, inventory, rewards, currency, gacha, shop, rankings, and match history.
  • Public lobby, public room list, matchmaking, queues, MMR, auto-fill, and bots.
  • Free-text chat, moderation tools, kick/ban, reporting, host transfer UI, and room ownership persistence.
  • Mid-match setting changes, custom room rules, configurable turn timers, best-of-5, and custom maps.
  • New playable characters beyond Nova, Vesper, Kaelii, and Perlah.
  • Friendly-fire room setting and full allied damage mode.
  • Second weapons, special shots, passives, upgrades, ultimates, and character stat sheets.
  • Advanced wind bands, local gravity zones, gravity wells as map hazards, and map-specific physics rules.
  • Mobile/touch, gamepad, remappable controls, and full accessibility pass.
  • Voice barks, KO shouts, music, ambient audio, cosmetic SFX, alternate VFX, and character audio identity pass.
  • Full spectator mode, spectator camera controls, spectator emotes, waiting room, and mid-match join spectators.
  • Interactive tutorial, practice range, polished launch UX, public marketing page, and trailer/social-media polish.

Decision Log

Decision Reason
V1 is playtest alpha, not full release. Reliable online 2v2 must be proven before accounts, economy, and public discovery.
1v1 is supported but not the primary promise. It helps practical playtesting without replacing the 2v2 target.
No public matchmaking in v1. Private rooms are enough to get real feedback from friends and early testers.
No durable rewards in v1. Session-only playtest alpha does not need accounts or inventory.
No new playable characters in v1. Four existing characters are enough to test 2v2, and extra roster content would multiply balance and asset work.
Weapon names are working names. Behavior matters more than final copy while the v1 contract is being finalized.

Change Control

After approval, the v1 design can be clarified often but changed rarely.

Allowed Without Contract Change

  • Fixing wording, typos, or ambiguity.
  • Adding implementation notes that match existing scope.
  • Recording discoveries, risks, or playtest notes.
  • Adding ideas to the V2 parking lot.
  • Tuning numeric values that are explicitly marked as tuning values.

Requires Explicit Contract Change

  • Adding or removing v1 features.
  • Changing supported modes, match rules, room rules, or acceptance gates.
  • Adding playable characters, accounts, persistence, public matchmaking, or economy systems.
  • Changing the core one-weapon identity of a v1 character.
To change v1, the user must explicitly say: "I am requesting a v1 contract change." Codex should push back, ask what v1 acceptance test fails without the change, require a tradeoff or deferral, and record the decision before editing locked scope.
